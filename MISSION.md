# Mission

BrandTrace’s mission is to put clear, source-based information about products and the companies behind them back into consumers’ hands.

We build a trusted consumer ownership and product-transparency database so people can see who owns the brands they buy, how products are made and labeled, and what evidence supports those claims — then decide for themselves.

## Long-Term Vision

Consumers should be able to understand:

- The ownership relationships behind everyday products (brands, parent companies, acquisitions)
- Documented product attributes that matter to health and values — including organic, non-GMO, heirloom, farm-to-table, and related certifications when sources support them
- Differences in formulation or labeling across markets when those differences are documented
- Which local and independent businesses operate with unusual transparency

BrandTrace connects products, brands, companies, ownership history, and evidence in a way that is careful, verifiable, and usable at the point of decision.

## What We Stand For

- **Information as leverage.** Large organizations already use detailed data. Consumers deserve the same clarity about ownership and product composition.
- **Natural and responsible production, as facts — not slogans.** We help people identify brands and producers that document organic, non-GMO, heirloom, farm-to-table, and similar practices. We also document, with sources, when products include additives, preservatives, or packaging materials that raise documented concerns. We do not invent claims; we surface what can be verified.
- **Local and independent businesses.** We spotlight producers and retailers that practice real transparency, especially farm-to-table and community-scale food businesses, so others can learn from them.
- **Accountability without a script.** Accountability means visible ownership and visible evidence — not BrandTrace telling people whom to boycott or which party to blame.

## Commitments

BrandTrace commits to:

- Consumer transparency without political direction
- Source-based documentation of ownership and product claims
- Clear separation between verified information, pending review, community submissions, and opinion
- Community participation with review safeguards
- A free core experience for users
- Neutral language and careful presentation
- Long-term data integrity

## Success Standard

BrandTrace succeeds when ordinary people, researchers, journalists, and businesses can rely on it as a careful, well-sourced reference — and when that information helps households choose products and companies that match their own values, including preferences for natural, organic, non-GMO, and farm-to-table options when the evidence supports those labels.
