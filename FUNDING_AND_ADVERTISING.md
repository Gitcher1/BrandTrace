# Funding and Advertising Policy

BrandTrace is and will remain free for all users.

This document defines how the project may be funded and what kinds of advertising are acceptable if the project grows large enough to need revenue.

## Core Commitments

1. **Always free for end users**  
   Scanning, product lookup, local records, viewing verified ownership information, and core transparency features must remain free. No paywalls, freemium gates, or paid “verification” tiers for consumers.

2. **Mission alignment required**  
   Any revenue source must support — or at least not undermine — the goals of:
   - Consumer transparency
   - Corporate and small-business accountability
   - Growth and visibility of independent and small businesses that operate transparently

3. **No conflict of interest with verified data**  
   Advertising or sponsorship must never influence which ownership claims are marked verified, how evidence is presented, or the ranking of records.

## Acceptable Future Revenue (when needed)

- Advertising that promotes independent, small, or transparent businesses
- Advertising for tools, services, or education that help small businesses or increase accountability
- Grants, donations, or sponsorships from aligned organizations (clearly disclosed)
- Optional paid services that do **not** restrict core free functionality (for example, advanced self-hosted support or high-volume API access for research institutions) — only if introduced carefully and without harming the free consumer experience

## Prohibited or Restricted

- Ads that promote large corporations currently under accountability scrutiny in a way that creates real or apparent conflict
- Native advertising or sponsored content presented as verified BrandTrace data
- Any payment that buys preferential treatment in the verified database
- Dark patterns, tracking-heavy ad networks that conflict with the project’s privacy stance, or ads that undermine trust

## Disclosure

If advertising or sponsorship is ever introduced:

- It will be clearly labeled
- A public list of current sponsors/advertisers will be maintained
- The distinction between paid placement and verified ownership records will remain absolute

## Review

This policy may be updated as the project grows. Material changes should be discussed openly with the community and documented in the repository history.

Ember Fire Media retains responsibility for enforcing this policy until a broader governance structure is established (see GOVERNANCE.md).
