# Project Philosophy

BrandTrace exists to increase transparency.

Transparency creates accountability. Accountability creates trust. Trust creates better markets — and gives ordinary people more real choice.

## Guiding Statement

> We document.
>
> We verify.
>
> Consumers decide.

## Why This Matters

Many households feel the gap between the information available to large institutions and the information available at the checkout. Cost of living, product complexity, and opaque ownership make it hard to know who you are actually supporting. BrandTrace exists to narrow that gap with facts, not slogans.

We care especially about food and everyday consumer goods: farm-to-table producers, organic and non-GMO claims, heirloom and traditional production, and documented differences in ingredients or packaging across markets. Those topics matter because they affect health, local economies, and trust. They are still handled the same way as everything else on the platform: **with sources, status labels, and no political prescription.**

## Neutrality Standard

BrandTrace is a factual ownership and product-transparency database. It is not:

- A political platform
- A corporate attack platform
- A boycott application
- A place for unsupported accusations

We present documented information neutrally. Consumers use that information according to their own values — including preferences for natural, organic, non-GMO, or local production.

Regulatory criticism, international formulation differences, and additive or packaging concerns may appear when they are **documented with reliable sources**. They are never presented as campaign talking points or as BrandTrace’s official political position.

## Evidence Before Claims

No unsupported claim should ever be presented as fact. Every factual statement should be verifiable whenever possible. Records should include reliable sources that support:

- Ownership relationships, acquisitions, and corporate structures
- Product identity and label data
- Certifications and production claims (organic, non-GMO, etc.) when cited
- Documented formulation or packaging differences where relevant

## Information Classifications

BrandTrace must distinguish clearly between:

- **Verified Information** — reviewed against reliable sources under the verification protocol
- **Pending Review** — being evaluated before publication as verified
- **Community Submission** — user-provided information not yet verified
- **Opinion** — subjective commentary that must never be confused with verified fact
- **Spotlight** — featured local or independent businesses recognized for transparency practices (clearly labeled as spotlight, not as a universal ranking)

## Monthly Local Business Spotlight

Each month BrandTrace may feature a local or independent business recommended by users, highlighting concrete transparency practices others can learn from (sourcing, labeling, ownership clarity, farm-to-table relationships, etc.). Spotlight entries:

- Are nominations from the community, reviewed before publication
- Celebrate specific, describable practices — not vague praise
- Do not imply that non-featured businesses are “bad”
- Remain separate from the verified ownership database

## Design Questions

Before implementing any feature, ask:

1. Does this increase transparency?
2. Does this improve trust?
3. Does this empower consumers?
4. Can this information be verified?
5. Will this still make sense five years from now?

If not, redesign it.
